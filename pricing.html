What's included

Everything you need to run a coaching practice.

Tap any feature for a plain-English explanation of what it does for you.

AI & Coaching Intelligence

  • AI analyzes every session to show you what landed with your client, what you missed, and patterns over time. See exactly what's working.
  • Track how each client evolves across all your sessions. Spot patterns you'd never see on your own.
  • Walk into every session with a one-page brief on what your client said last time, what shifted, and what to revisit. No more flipping through old notes.
  • Upload a recording or paste a transcript and we structure it into searchable session notes. Your full history, in one place.
  • Auto-formatted session notes in GROW, CLEAR, or OSKAR — or build your own template. Stop reinventing the page every time.

Client Experience

  • Send clients structured intake before the first session and lightweight check-ins between sessions. They walk in ready to work.
  • Goals, milestones, and progress markers your clients can see. Their wins are visible to both of you.
  • Two-way messaging that doesn't get lost in your inbox or theirs. Everything threaded by client.
  • Clients book themselves through your scheduler. Automated SMS and email reminders so no-shows stop being a thing.
  • Connect your Zoom account once. Every booking gets its own meeting link automatically — no manual scheduling.
  • Bookings flow into your Google Calendar in real time. Cancel or reschedule from either side and both stay in sync.

Business Tools

  • Your own page on the marketplace, searchable by specialty. Clients find you instead of the other way around.
  • Sell session bundles with credit tracking. Issue coupons for promotions or referrals. All ledgered cleanly.
  • Take card payments directly. Funds land in your Stripe account. Zero commission — your revenue stays yours.
  • Create and sell courses with lessons, quizzes, and certificates. Turn your expertise into passive income.
  • Send polished proposals to prospective clients in minutes. Close the deal without leaving the platform.
  • Request, collect, and display client testimonials right on your profile page. Social proof that compounds over time.
